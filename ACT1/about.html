About Kazakhstan

Geography

Kazakhstan is the largest landlocked country in the world, known for its endless steppes, dramatic mountain ranges like the Tian Shan, and vast deserts. It sits at the heart of Central Asia, bridging Europe and Asia.

Languages

>The official state language is Kazakh, but Russian is widely spoken in daily life, business, and education, making it a bilingual nation.

Culture

Kazakhstan has deep nomadic roots — hospitality, horse culture, yurts, and traditional games reflect its rich heritage. Modern Kazakhstan combines this legacy with futuristic cityscapes, especially in its capital, Astana.
