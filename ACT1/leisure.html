Leisure & Delicacies

Leisure Activities

  • Horseback riding across the Kazakh steppes.
  • Winter sports at Shymbulak Ski Resort.
  • Exploring historic Silk Road cities and architecture.
  • Hiking in the Altai Mountains and Tian Shan ranges.
  • Wildlife watching and eco-tours in national parks.

Famous Delicacies

  • Beshbarmak – the national dish of boiled meat and pasta squares.
  • Kumis – fermented mare’s milk with a unique tangy flavor.
  • Baursaks – fluffy fried dough, often enjoyed with tea.
  • Shashlik – skewered meat grilled over an open flame.
