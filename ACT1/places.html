Top 5 Must-Visit Places

Astana (Nur-Sultan)

The dazzling capital city of Kazakhstan, Astana is a perfect blend of futuristic architecture and rich traditions. Marvel at the iconic Baiterek Tower, stroll through the vast green spaces, and experience the luxury shopping and indoor beach at Khan Shatyr. The city skyline offers a stunning contrast between modern skyscrapers and elegant public spaces.

Almaty

Nestled at the foot of the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains, Almaty is Kazakhstan’s largest city and cultural heartbeat. Wander around its stylish coffee shops, soak in panoramic views from Kok Tobe hill, and don’t miss the peaceful Big Almaty Lake. Whether you're a foodie, art lover, or mountain hiker, Almaty has something to offer every curious traveler.

Charyn Canyon

Nicknamed the "Grand Canyon’s little brother," Charyn Canyon stretches for 154 km, revealing millions of years of geological beauty. Hike through the Valley of Castles, where wind and water have sculpted magnificent red rock formations. The canyon is a must-visit for adventurers, photographers, and anyone in search of breathtaking landscapes.

Lake Kaindy

This surreal sunken forest is a hidden gem located in the Kolsai Lakes National Park. Lake Kaindy's partially submerged pine trees rise out of turquoise waters, creating an otherworldly landscape. It's a paradise for divers, hikers, and nature photographers seeking peace and raw beauty far from crowded tourist paths.

Turkistan

Known as the spiritual heart of Kazakhstan, Turkistan offers a journey back in time. The Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi, a UNESCO World Heritage site, stands as a majestic masterpiece of Timurid architecture. The city is rich in Islamic heritage, ancient trade history, and is a significant pilgrimage site that offers a deep cultural experience.
